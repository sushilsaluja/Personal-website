About Sushil

A career in the City, now in its service.

Sushil Saluja is Alderman for Coleman Street Ward and the City of London’s Lead Member for Innovation, Technology & AI.

He has spent over 30 years building a distinguished career in financial services, technology and professional services, primarily in the City of London. Before moving to a portfolio career, Sushil spent 32 years at Accenture, where he was Senior Partner for the $4bn Financial Services EMEA business, with responsibility for more than 30,000 people.

He previously led Accenture’s Financial Services practices across Europe, Asia Pacific, and the UK & Ireland, and worked in over 40 countries, including three years based in Hong Kong. Sushil also served as Head of Technology at the Bank of England and is a founding board member of TheCityUK.

The eldest of two children, Sushil and his sister learned the importance of community, education and giving back from their parents. It is something he shares with his wife Seema, his sister Melanie and his mother, who still teaches community yoga several times a week despite now being over 80 years old!

What I’m working on for the ward and the City

Five areas of focus for the businesses, workers, residents and charities of Coleman Street and the wider Square Mile.

My commitment

Share regular updates, report on progress, and stay accessible to residents, workers and businesses.

01

Maintain the City’s leadership in financial & professional services

  • Enhancing competitiveness through simpler regulation
  • Speeding up new technology and AI adoption
  • Attracting high-tech firms, scale-ups and listings
  • Promoting English Common Law, so more business contracts are written in London

As City Lead Member for Innovation, Technology & AI.

02

Help smaller businesses flourish

  • Minimising red tape
  • Better understanding the challenges small businesses face
  • Identifying how the City can better support SMEs to succeed
  • Making livery companies better understood and more accessible

The City’s economy depends on a thriving business community, including SMEs.

03

Nurture talent & opportunities in the City

  • A greater focus on talent, including helping students find summer placements with City firms
  • Wider access to mentoring and networking

By boosting our talent we can help the City thrive.

  • Keeping the City an attractive destination, with access to heritage places
  • Keeping the City a place for vibrant evening activities
  • Supporting carbon net zero by 2027 for the City’s own operations, and by 2040 for the Square Mile

Environmental sustainability is the biggest issue of our lifetimes.

05

Make the City more inclusive & accessible for all

  • Encouraging volunteering
  • Celebrating local heritage
  • Improving the accessibility of local City spaces

Building a sense of community and a more welcoming City.

Sushil’s role

Sushil is Alderman for the ward of Coleman Street and the City Lead Member for Innovation, Technology & AI. As Alderman, he:

  • Promotes the City of London internationally
  • Helps drive change to keep the City competitive and relevant
  • Represents the businesses, residents and charities of the ward
  • Takes an active part in civic life, including support for the livery companies

He is also Chairman of the SME charity Heart of the City and a founding board member of TheCityUK.

Governance

  • The City of London Corporation is “older than Parliament”, with the Lord Mayor as its formal head
  • Much of its expenditure is funded from historical and endowment funds, reducing pressure on the public purse
  • Each ward elects Common Councillors every four years and one Alderman every six years, each largely non-party-political
  • Future Sheriffs and Lord Mayors are selected from the Court of Aldermen (25 elected Aldermen)
Common Councillors
  • Oversee the City’s work through the Court of Common Council
  • Serve on committees covering policy areas such as planning and finance
Aldermen
  • Promote the City internationally and help drive change
  • Support civic life and the livery companies

Coleman Street Ward

Named after the charcoal burners who lived there in medieval times, Coleman Street Ward is one of the 25 wards that make up the historic core of London.

The poet John Keats is believed to have been born on or near Coleman Street, where his grandfather owned an inn.

Liverpool Street’s Crossrail platforms run directly beneath Finsbury Circus, which is why the Gardens were closed for so long during construction.
